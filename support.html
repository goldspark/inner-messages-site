Support

One person builds this app, and that person answers the mail. Write to the address below and you'll hear back.

Quick answers

Nothing happens when I open the app

Inner Messages only works between two people. Until someone accepts your request there is nothing to receive. Share your Inner ID with your partner or a family member and ask them to add you.

My partner isn't getting my messages

Check that you are connected to each other in Requests, and that they have notifications enabled for Inner Messages in their phone settings. Messages arrive as a surprise, so they do not show up the second you write them — that is the point.

I forgot my password

Use the password reset page. The link lands in the inbox you signed up with.

Write to me

Bugs, confusing bits, feature ideas, or anything about your account — all welcome. If you are reporting something broken, it helps to say which phone you have and what you were doing when it happened.
