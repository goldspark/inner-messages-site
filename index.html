How it works

Three steps, then you forget about it.

That's the point. You write when you have a quiet minute. They receive it later, when they are not expecting anything at all.

1

Connect with your people

Share your Inner ID with your partner or a family member and accept their request. The app only works between two people who both agreed to it.

2

Write ahead of time

Leave a note for them whenever it crosses your mind — a few words, an inside joke, something you would say if you were in the same room.

3

It shows up as a surprise

Your message arrives on their phone later, on its own. They never know which day it comes, and that is what makes it land.
